# Red Letter

Words spoken by God and Jesus in the King James Bible, one at a time, written to you.
Tell it how you feel and it finds the verses that fit, with prayer points to pray over them.

Built by Otti.

- Every verse is the King James Version (public domain), checked word for word.
- Red marks only what God or Jesus says; narration stays grey.
- Works offline once opened, and can be added to your phone's home screen.

Fonts: Bodoni Moda, Spectral and Archivo, all under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
